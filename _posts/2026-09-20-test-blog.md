**This is a test post for previewing the blog.** It is sample content, not a report of lab research. Remove or replace it before publishing the blog.

## The question

How can lab members share research and project updates in a format that is
easy to read, review, and maintain alongside the existing lab website?

A useful write-up should explain the problem, show the approach, and distinguish
what has been learned from what still needs investigation.

## Our approach

This blog prototype uses the site's existing typography, colors, and Markdown
publishing system. Each post has a short summary, an author, and space for
figures and links to project resources.

<figure markdown="0">
  <img src="/assets/images/blog/test-blog.svg" alt="Three connected stages: write a post, review it with a lab member, and share it publicly." width="720" height="400">
  <figcaption>Figure 1. The proposed publishing process: members write a post, request lab feedback, and merge it for publication after approval.</figcaption>
</figure>

Review happens through pull requests. That keeps the publishing process in the
same place as the website's code and lets reviewers suggest specific edits.

## What we can assess so far

The prototype shows how project summaries, author profiles, and a figure can
fit into the existing site. It has not been evaluated with lab members, so we
cannot yet say whether the writing and review process meets their needs.

For an actual research post, this section would describe the evidence:
observations, results, or measurements, together with the limitations of the
study or prototype.

## Next steps

- Try the format with a real project write-up.
- Ask another lab member to review both the content and the publishing steps.
- Refine the template based on that feedback before publishing.
